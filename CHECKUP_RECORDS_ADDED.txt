PERIODIC CHECKUP RECORDS ADDED

Flutter:
- lib/screens/checkup_records_screen.dart
- lib/models/checkup_record.dart
- Checkup navigation button in the Food & Water app bar
- Manual add, edit, delete, refresh, and validation

Backend:
- POST   /api/checkup-records
- GET    /api/checkup-records
- GET    /api/checkup-records/:id
- PATCH  /api/checkup-records/:id
- DELETE /api/checkup-records/:id

Storage:
- backend-node/data/checkup_records/checkup_records.json
- Test records persist after restarting the Node.js backend and computer.
- This local JSON storage is for development testing and can be replaced by MongoDB later.

Required input:
- Checkup date
- At least one measurement

Optional measurements:
- eGFR
- Serum creatinine
- UACR
- Systolic blood pressure
- Diastolic blood pressure
- Blood glucose
- Notes
