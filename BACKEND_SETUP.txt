BACKEND ADD-ON FOR AN EXISTING FLUTTER PROJECT

Copy these folders into the ROOT of your current Flutter project:

- backend-node/
- ml-fastapi/
- docs/

Do not copy them into lib/, android/, or ios/.

Your resulting workspace can look like:

XIAOMI_WEARABLE_TEST/
├── android/
├── ios/
├── lib/
├── linux/
├── macos/
├── web/
├── windows/
├── backend-node/
├── ml-fastapi/
└── docs/
